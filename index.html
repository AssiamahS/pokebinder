How buying works

  1. Pick a card

    If it has an eBay listing you check out there, with eBay's money back guarantee. Otherwise send an offer.

  2. See the real one

    Ask for front, back and corner photos with your name on a note. Graded cards come with the cert number to look up.

  3. Pay with protection

    • It ships tracked

    Cash sales only, no trades for store credit. Conditions use the TCGplayer scale: Near Mint, Lightly Played, Moderately Played, Heavily Played, Damaged.
