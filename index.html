How buying works

1. Pick a card

Cards with an eBay listing check out on eBay, with eBay's money back guarantee. Everything else is by direct offer.

2. See it first

Ask for front, back and corner photos, or a timestamped photo with your name on a note. Graded cards come with the cert number so you can look it up.

3. Pay with protection

    4. Shipping

    Cash only on the seller side: no trades for store credit. Conditions follow the TCGplayer scale (NM, LP, MP, HP, DMG).
